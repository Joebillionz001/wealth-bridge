    Privacy Policy

    Last Updated: January 2024 | We take your privacy seriously

    1. Introduction

    Your privacy is important to us. It is WealthBridge's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you through our Platform. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of your personal data.

    2. Information We Collect

    We collect information for legitimate business purposes and your benefit:

    • Account Information: Name, email, phone number, address (for KYC compliance)
    • Financial Information: Banking details, transaction history, investment preferences
    • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, cookies
    • Usage Data: How you interact with our Platform, pages visited, time spent

    3. How We Use Your Information

    We use your information to:

    • Provide and maintain our investment services
    • Process transactions and send related information
    • Verify your identity and prevent fraud
    • Send promotional materials and updates (with your consent)
    • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
    • Comply with legal obligations and regulations
    • Improve our Platform and develop new services

    4. Data Security

    We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information: AES-256 encryption for data in transit and at rest, multi-signature wallet technology, regular security audits by third parties, and strict access controls. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    5. Data Retention

    We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Account information is retained for the duration of your account and for 7 years after closure for regulatory compliance. Transaction records are retained for 10 years as required by financial regulations.

    6. Your Rights

    You have the right to:

    • Access your personal information
    • Correct inaccurate data
    • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
    • Opt-out of marketing communications
    • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

    7. Third-Party Sharing

    We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only with: payment processors (for transactions), regulatory authorities (for compliance), and service providers under strict confidentiality agreements. We ensure all third parties maintain the same level of data protection as WealthBridge.

    8. Contact Us

    If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@wealthbridge.com or through our contact form.
