    Common Issues

    Why is my withdrawal pending?

    Withdrawals typically process within 24-48 hours. Bank transfers may take 1-3 business days. Check your bank account and spam folder for confirmation emails.

    How long does KYC verification take?

    KYC verification typically takes 24-48 hours. During peak periods, it may take up to 5 business days. We'll notify you via email when verified.

    Can I change my investment while it's active?

    No, once an investment is active, you cannot change it. However, you can withdraw your funds anytime (minus any applicable fees) and start a new investment.
