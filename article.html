Strategy 1: Stay the Course

It's tempting to sell when markets are down, but history shows that markets recover. Sticking to your long-term investment plan is often the most prudent course of action.

Strategy 2: Rebalance Your Portfolio

Volatility can shift your asset allocation. Rebalancing—selling some assets that have performed well and buying more of those that have underperformed—helps you maintain your desired risk level.
