    Detailed Guides

    How do I create an account? ▼
    1. Click on "Sign Up" button on the homepage
    2. Enter your email address and create a strong password
    3. Verify your email by clicking the verification link
    4. Complete your profile information
    5. You're ready to start investing!
    What payment methods do you accept? ▼

    We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience:

    • 💳 Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard)
    • 🏦 Bank Transfers
    • ₿ Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum)
    • 💻 E-Wallets (PayPal, Skrill)

    All payments are encrypted and secured with SSL 256-bit encryption.

    What is the minimum deposit amount? ▼

    The minimum deposit amount is $100 for all investment plans. This allows us to ensure effective fund management and better returns for our investors.

    💡 Tip: You can increase your deposit at any time to boost your returns!

    How long does withdrawal take? ▼

    Withdrawal processing times depend on your chosen method:

    • Bank Transfer: 2-5 business days
    • Credit/Debit Card: 1-3 business days
    • Cryptocurrency: 1-2 hours (network dependent)
    • E-Wallet: 24 hours

    All withdrawals are processed during business hours (Monday-Friday).

    How does the referral program work? ▼

    Our referral program allows you to earn commissions from each referred investor:

    1. Share your unique referral link with friends
    2. They sign up using your link
    3. You earn commission on their deposits
    4. Earn more as you reach higher tier levels

    Commission Tiers:

    • Basic: 10% commission
    • Silver: 15% commission (5+ referrals)
    • Gold: 20% commission (20+ referrals)
    How do I enable two-factor authentication? ▼

    Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security to your account:

    1. Go to Settings > Security Settings
    2. Click "Enable 2FA"
    3. Download an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.)
    4. Scan the QR code with your app
    5. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm
    6. Save your backup codes in a safe place

    ⚠️ Important: Store your backup codes securely. You'll need them if you lose access to your authenticator.
