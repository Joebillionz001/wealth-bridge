Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start investing?

To start, simply sign up for an account, deposit funds into your wallet, and choose an investment plan from your dashboard. The process is designed to be quick and straightforward.

How are my profits calculated?

Profits are calculated based on the weekly ROI (Return on Investment) of your chosen plan. For example, a 5% weekly ROI on a $100 investment will yield $5 in profit each week.

Is my money secure?

Yes, security is our top priority. While this is a prototype platform using simulated data, a real-world version would employ industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your funds and data.

Can I withdraw my money at any time?

You can withdraw funds from your main balance at any time. Funds committed to an active investment plan are locked until the investment period is complete.
