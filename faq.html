Frequently Asked Questions

What is WealthBridge?

WealthBridge is a premier investment platform that allows individuals to grow their wealth through expertly managed portfolios in Real Estate, Technology, Cryptocurrency, and Sustainable projects.

How do I start investing?

Starting is easy! Simply create an account, deposit funds into your wallet using our secure payment gateway, and browse our "Investment Plans" to choose a plan that suits your financial goals.

What is the minimum deposit amount?

You can start investing with as little as $20 depending on the plan you choose. Our "Crypto Beginner Fund" is designed for entry-level investors.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawal requests are processed within 24 hours. Depending on your withdrawal method (Bank Transfer or Crypto Wallet), funds usually arrive instantly or within 1-3 business days.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your personal data and financial transactions. We do not share your data with third parties without your consent.

How does the referral program work?

When you invite a friend using your unique referral link, you earn a percentage of their first deposit as a bonus. You can track your referrals and earnings directly from your dashboard.
