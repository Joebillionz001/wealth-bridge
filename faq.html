    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about WealthBridge, our investment plans, and how to get started.

    Getting Started

    How do I create an account?

    Click "Sign Up" on our website, enter your email and create a secure password. Verify your email address, and you're ready to start investing. The entire process takes less than 5 minutes.

    What documents do I need to verify my identity?

    We require a valid government-issued ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID) and proof of address. This ensures compliance with global financial regulations and protects your account from unauthorized access.

    Is there a minimum deposit amount?

    The minimum initial deposit is $100. However, different investment plans may have their own minimum requirements. We offer flexible options for investors of all sizes.

    Investments & Returns

    How much can I earn with WealthBridge?

    Returns vary based on your chosen investment plan and market conditions. Our plans typically offer 5-15% monthly returns, with some specialized plans offering higher potential. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results.

    When do I receive my returns?

    Returns are calculated daily and credited directly to your account. You can view your earnings in real-time on your dashboard. Most plans allow daily or weekly withdrawals of profits.

    Can I withdraw my principal before plan maturity?

    Early withdrawal options depend on your chosen plan. Some plans allow penalty-free early withdrawals, while others may charge a small fee (typically 5-10%). Check your plan details for specific terms.

    Security & Safety

    Is my money safe on WealthBridge?

    Yes. We use AES-256 encryption, multi-signature wallets, and keep 95% of funds in cold storage. All transactions are verified through our advanced security protocols. We conduct regular third-party security audits.

    What security features does my account have?

    Your account includes two-factor authentication (2FA), encrypted passwords, activity logs, and IP whitelisting. You can set up security alerts for all account activities and transactions.

    What happens if I lose my password?

    You can reset your password through the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. We'll send a secure reset link to your registered email. For security, you'll need to verify your identity.

    Withdrawals & Payments

    How do I withdraw my profits?

    Go to your Dashboard, click "Withdraw", select your desired amount and payment method. Enter your banking details, confirm the transaction, and your funds will be processed within 24 hours.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept bank transfers, credit/debit cards, digital wallets (PayPal, Google Pay, Apple Pay), and major cryptocurrencies. Each method offers different processing times and fees.

    Are there any withdrawal fees?

    WealthBridge doesn't charge withdrawal fees. However, your bank or payment provider may apply standard fees. Cryptocurrency withdrawals may have network fees that are clearly displayed before confirmation.

    Referrals & Bonuses

    How does the referral program work?

    Share your unique referral code with friends and family. When they sign up using your code and make their first deposit, you earn a commission (typically 10% of their investment). There's no limit to how much you can earn!

    What's the referral commission rate?

    Standard referral commission is 10% of each referred user's initial deposit. However, we have tiered rewards: earn 12% for 5+ referrals, 15% for 10+ referrals, and up to 20% for 20+ referrals per month.

    Didn't find your answer?

    Our support team is available 24/7 to help you with any questions.
